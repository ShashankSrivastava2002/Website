How I work

Almost anyone can call an LLM API. What I build is the boring, load-bearing half around it — the layer that decides whether an agent is safe to ship: does it stay in budget, can you trace why it failed, and does "success" actually mean success?

Production thinking
Evals, cost-per-token, and crash-resilient state — treated as first-class, not afterthoughts.
Root-cause depth
I chase failures to their source — a reversed guardrail check, a silently-ignored reasoning kwarg, a lost auth token across boundaries.
Honest measurement
Outcome-classification pipelines that grade every production run, not vanity numbers.
